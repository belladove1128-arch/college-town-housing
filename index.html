How to read this page

Only completed sales priced at $10,000 or more are included. The rule removes implausibly low values identified in the source notes and applies consistently to every figure. Medians use individual sale records; repeated addresses are retained because they can represent different units or transactions.

Data notes

Campus areas use each property's nearest university landmark. Distance in the source is straight-line, not driving distance. City and ZIP labels may not always align with coordinates. Home type is omitted because the source warns that it is frequently misclassified. Values are not independently verified against county records.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Collected March 2026.Median prices apply the $10,000 minimum sale-price rule.
